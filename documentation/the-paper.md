# The Paper

The canonical version is **v3.1**: _The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis: A Teleological-Recursive Simulation Framework (v3.1)_, by Walter Hivoina, Independent researcher.

Read it on Zenodo: [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23464415](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23464415)

## What it claims

Most simulation arguments ask _whether_ reality is simulated. GPH asks _what for_: a simulated reality may function as a developmental curriculum for consciousness, with a graduation condition that is collective and recursive — intelligence creates intelligence, then recognizes the pattern of creation in itself.

Four axioms define the framework: developmental purpose, productive pressure, recursive threshold, and observable consequence.

The laser-interface idea is an optional conjecture, not the core: the read-head model (wall as storage medium, laser as read head, altered mind as decoder, across seven variables) is testable on its own. The falsifiability discipline holds throughout — a positive laser result counts as a controllable perceptual effect only, never as proof of simulation or curriculum, and a clean null kills the interface claim while the core thesis stands or falls on its own disconfirmers (D1–D4). Provisional graduation criteria C1–C4 are stated in §05.
