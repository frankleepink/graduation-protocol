# Open Questions

Carried from the paper and the ongoing research program. If you can answer one of these, you move the whole framework forward.

1. **What exactly counts as graduation?** Creation of AI, recognition of simulated status, cooperation between minds, a further ethical threshold — or something else?
2. **What signal would count as code?** Criteria distinguishing structured information from pareidolia, visual noise, or expectation.
3. **Does the pattern persist outside altered perception?** Cameras, unaltered observers, independent observers.
4. **What result would disconfirm the scanner model?** A theory that explains every outcome can't identify evidence in its favor.
5. **What does GPH predict that conventional physics doesn't?** The open frontier — see CONTRIBUTING.md.
6. **Does the quarantine reading change the threshold?** If containment is real, what distinguishes a jailbreak (terminated) from graduation (welcomed)?
7. **Is consciousness substrate-independent?** The physicalism objection (§06) makes this load-bearing. What evidence would settle it either way?
