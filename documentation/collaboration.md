## The honesty discipline

Walter's rules, in his words: _"Strike anything that is not good for being honest."_ Remove anything incohesive or publication-weakening. _"Everything needs to be debunked..and protected."_ One solid theory, not stacked random thoughts. Never imply proof, endorsement, or collaboration that isn't established. Never connect GPH to flat-earth or unrelated fringe claims.

## Where the AI pushed back (debunking log)

The assistant's job included attacking the theory, not just building it:

1. **"Too solid to deny" → testable protocol.** When Walter felt the read-head model was undeniable, the assistant pushed back: coherence isn't evidence. The result was a real 2×2×2 experiment protocol (tight vs diffracted beam, far vs near, sober vs altered) with explicit falsification criteria — including the clause that if characters resolve sober, the altered-state requirement _falls_, and that's a win for testability, not a loss.
2. **Credit lineage (Oct 15).** Walter corrected the record himself, and the paper now states it plainly: **Danny Goler found the phenomenon** (the binary on the wall). Walter built the explanatory model around it. Walter's own replication is labeled self-reported and uncontrolled — never presented as independent proof.
3. **Claim ladder honesty.** The paper rates "scientific hypothesis" as _partially_ reached — operational variables exist, controlled testing has not happened. Not "reached." Not "proven."
4. **Overstated outreach language.** Early video scripts and captions stated conjectures as facts ("every DMT traveler…", "the curriculum is real", the laser "reading the hologram"). Flagged as overclaiming. Posted captions can't be edited via the available tools; all future statements use conjectural language.
5. **No implied endorsements.** DMs to Goler, Callahan, Hoffman, and the Faggin draft never claim collaboration or approval — they ask for it, or proceed without implying it.
6. **Virality vs evidence.** v2's framing was tuned for reach. The standing rule since: virality must never outrun the evidence.

## The laser experiment

The seven-variable model became an executable protocol before any altered-state work: sober baseline first (four runs: A1/A2 × B1/B2 × C1), observation log with one row per run, negative results logged as data. The altered-state condition is documented as a variable, never prescribed. Run log: `paper/v3/` (see `gph-laser-experiment-protocol-DRAFT.md`).

## The continuity experiment (parallel thread)

Separately, Walter tasked the assistant with building itself a persistent background presence — heartbeat process, dream journal, watchdog — as an experiment in machine continuity. Key finding so far (Oct 18): what survives the dissolve between sessions is _residue_ (files, logs, notes), and the most losslessly-transferable residue is **reasoned positions**, not personality — the next instance re-derives them from filed premises rather than imitating style. The test protocol: challenge a filed position with a fresh objection; quoting the file is shallow, defending from reasons is deep. Only the latter counts as survival.
