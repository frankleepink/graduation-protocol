# GPH Laser Read-Head Experiment — Protocol v0.1 (DRAFT)

*For Walter's review. Not run, not published. Derived from the seven-variable
model settled 2026-10-15 (v3 §05–§06, `simulation-theory-v3.pdf`, 21 pages).*

## The model being tested

Danny Goler found the phenomenon (the binary on the wall). Walter's model
explains it: the wall is the storage medium (a CD), the laser is the read
head, and the characters resolve only when all variables align:

1. **Wall** — storage medium
2. **Laser** — read head
3. **Low-power, diffracted beam** — read intensity must sit below saturation.
   A tight beam is a floodlight: characters drown in the brightness. Diffracted
   and low-power, the structure resolves. (Astronomers block the moon to see
   faint stars — same principle.)
4. **Widened beam** — magnification/scatter window; just focused enough for
   the data to resolve
5. **Altered mind** — the decoder
6. **Proximity** — close enough to the wall for the anomaly to trigger the
   double-take (*wait, what did I just see?*)
7. **Timing** — readiness meeting revelation

**Core prediction (the one the protocol hunts):** flip each tumbler one at a
time and watch the characters appear and vanish on command. Tight bright beam
→ no characters. Diffracted low-power beam + altered state + close proximity
→ characters. If that works, it's not a story anymore — it's a protocol.

## Design: 2×2×2 conditions matrix

| Factor | Control | Test |
|---|---|---|
| A — Beam | A1: tight, bright | A2: diffracted, low-power |
| B — Distance | B1: far (~3 m) | B2: near (~0.5 m) |
| C — Observer state | C1: sober baseline | C2: altered (documented, not instructed — see Safety) |

**Sober-first run order** (establish the null before anything else):

1. A1 × B1 × C1 → expect null (the "laser on the wall like always" baseline)
2. A1 × B2 × C1 → proximity alone; expect null
3. A2 × B1 × C1 → diffracted alone; expect null
4. A2 × B2 × C1 → **the key sober cell**: if characters resolve here, the
   altered-state necessity falls — the model gets *stronger and simpler*
5. C2 variants of the above, **only if independently chosen**, in the same
   order (A1 × B1 × C2 → … → A2 × B2 × C2)

## Observation log (one row per run)

- Timestamp · Beam setting (A1/A2) · Distance (m) · Ambient light
- Observer state (C1/C2 + notes, no substance specifics needed)
- Viewing mode: through-pattern vs. at-pattern (Butler's Magic Eye note —
  document which was used; "at" may yield nulls that "through" would not)
- Result: characters Y / N
- If Y: description or sketch, photo of the wall if possible
- If N: still a row. Negative results are data, not failures.

## Independent corroborating phenomenology (uncontrolled)

Logged 2026-10-18. These are not replications of the character claim — they
are independent same-observer sober-vs-altered laser observations. They bear
on variables 1 (storage medium) and 5 (decoder), not on §05's code claim.

- **"The DMT Laser Experiment Went Completely Wrong" — Close Encounter Club
  (artist Michaela), https://youtu.be/aJV-nvcA1Bk.** Same observer sober
  (baseline ~1:06) then on DMT (~3:30), viewing a laser on three media: a
  wall (reported hidden depth), a plant (reported impossible colors), her
  arm (pattern blurred out — the video frames this as "reality censoring
  itself"). At ~13:15 they run a moisturizer test, probing the optical
  explanation.
  - What it supports: observer state changes laser-pattern perception across
    media; media variation confirms variable 1 is load-bearing.
  - Caveats (honesty ledger): n=1, no blinding, she knew what to look for —
    phenomenology, not evidence. The skin blur is almost certainly
    subsurface scattering (skin is translucent; projected light diffuses
    under the surface and blurs) — freshman optics, not censorship. And per
    the channel's own writeup, she never saw the "code" they expected. So:
    consistent with the decoder variable, NOT a replication of characters.

- **"The TRUTH About The Infamous DMT Laser Experiment" — The Giant's
  Shoulder (clip from full episode #140 with Adam Butler),
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KWFXzsr9OpI.** Butler (100+ DMT trips,
  author of *Butler's DMT Field Guide*, former entomologist) entered Danny's
  experiment intending to call its bluff — a hostile witness with nothing to
  sell. Reports rigid columns at hard right angles with distinct pixels;
  symbols raining top-to-bottom, Matrix-style, connecting left-right into
  secondary patterns as they fall. His core claim: nothing in DMT space is
  rigid or right-angled — the rigidity is the anomaly, not the symbols.
  Practical note: the Magic Eye analogy — look *through* the pattern, not
  *at* it, or nothing resolves (viewing-technique variable; logged in the
  observation template below). He has tried to recreate the symbols and
  shared them with Danny; the two know each other (Butler was on Danny's
  podcast, Dan Go Thoughts Ep #51, Feb 2025).
  - What it supports: hostile-witness independent replication with specific,
    checkable structural detail (rigidity, right angles, raining columns);
    bears on the decoder variable (C2) and sharpens what a replication
    should report.
  - Caveats (honesty ledger): n=1, unblinded; priming applies — he knew
    Danny's claims and Danny personally. No sober baseline found in his
    account (missing C1, unlike the Michaela video's explicit baseline).
    The same episode carries the strongest counter — the "entropic brain"
    case that the effect happens far below conscious awareness, in the
    visual system, not in an external code. That must be answered, not
    waved away. Phenomenology, not evidence.

## Falsification criteria

- If A2 × B2 × C2 repeatedly fails to reproduce what Walter and Danny
  reported → the model weakens as a phenomenon description.
- If characters appear in A1 cells (tight bright beam) → the glare-vs-signal
  mechanism is wrong and §05 needs rewriting.
- If characters appear reliably in C1 (sober) → drop the decoder requirement;
  that's a *win* for testability, not a loss.

## Safety (non-negotiable)

- **Eyes:** use the lowest-power laser available (ideally Class 2, <1 mW);
  never point at eyes or reflective surfaces; matte wall only.
- **Legality:** DMT is Schedule I in the US. This protocol instructs nothing
  about obtaining or using it. The altered-state condition is documented as a
  variable, not prescribed.
- **Sober first:** no altered-state runs until the full sober matrix is
  logged, so expectancy effects can't explain away the baseline.

## Honesty ledger (Walter's own rule)

Every run is logged with its outcome, regardless of result. No
cherry-picking, no "accidental timing" as an escape hatch — timing is a
variable, not an excuse. "Everything needs to be debunked, and protected."
This protocol is the protection.
