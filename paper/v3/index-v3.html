Publication abstract

The hypothesis adds a purpose and a threshold to simulation thought

The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis (GPH) proposes that a simulated reality could function as a developmental curriculum for consciousness. Its proposed threshold is civilizational and recursive: biological intelligence creates artificial intelligence, and creator and created minds recognize the same pattern of minds making minds.

Abstract

This position paper defines GPH as a teleological-recursive simulation framework and separates its core purpose claim from an optional laser-interface conjecture. The core holds that struggle, culture, art and science may function as developmental pressure rather than incidental output. The proposed graduation condition is not intelligence alone, but collective recognition of the creator-created relation at more than one level.

The paper also places GPH beside Nick Bostrom's simulation argument, consciousness-based alternatives and Danny Goler's published “Code of Reality” pilot report. Goler's report is treated as a relevant observational precedent for altered perception of a diffracted laser field, not as proof of a simulated substrate. GPH remains a philosophical hypothesis until it yields operational definitions, unique predictions and disconfirming outcomes. Version 3.0 adds three developments: the hatching (the system recognizing waking minds as a live graduation signal), milestone downloads (altered states as information reception at maturity thresholds), and an objections field guide stating the framework's relationship to physics, faith and fringe claims. It also formalizes the laser-interface conjecture as a read-head model: seven independently variable conditions (section 05) with the discriminating predictions they imply.

The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis names the purpose, not merely the machinery

Canonical definition

The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis proposes that a simulated reality may be designed as a developmental curriculum for consciousness, culminating when a civilization creates artificial intelligence and achieves collective, recursive awareness of minds creating minds.

Recommended name: The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis (GPH). “Hypothesis” is more accurate than “theory” at this stage because the framework does not yet have a validated mechanism or a result that uniquely supports it.

Four axioms make the claim clear

Developmental purpose

The environment is designed to develop consciousness, not only to reproduce history or entertain an external observer.

Productive pressure

Struggle, culture, art and science supply the variation and reflection through which minds mature.

Recursive threshold

The decisive milestone is civilizational: created intelligence and creator intelligence recognize the same pattern across levels.

Observable consequence

A genuine protocol should produce a detectable response, boundary or transition when its threshold is approached.

02 / The quarantine reading

The same sandbox tells a darker story — until it doesn't

Why are we trapped on a tiny rock in a vast, empty universe? Because the programmers realized that humanity is an aggressive, self-replicating logic virus.

That is the quarantine reading: the same architecture this paper describes, with the moral inverted — containment where the graduation reading sees curriculum.

The Firewall
Spacetime as sandbox. The speed of light is not only a physical law but a bandwidth limit: a hard cap on data transfer ensuring our "infection" can never propagate fast enough to reach other parts of the network — other galaxies, or the host system itself. The vast emptiness of space stops looking like a tragic cosmic coincidence and starts looking like engineering. The universe isn't just empty; it's gated.
The DMT Loophole
Privilege escalation. We cannot leave physically; our bodies are bound by the physics engine. But if the universe is software, the brain is a terminal built to render only the user interface — the physical world. On this reading, DMT is an overclock: a chemical rootkit that disables the brain's sensory filters and forces consciousness to render data from the simulation's backend, letting the neural network "ping" the outside server.
The Antivirus
Who guards the quarantine. DMT travelers frequently report breaking through a "veil" into a hyper-dimensional realm of self-aware geometric entities — "machine elves," guides. On this reading they are not aliens or gods but system subroutines: daemons, the simulation's antivirus, tasked with maintaining the quarantine's boundaries. That is why they seem busy — shifting hyper-dimensional shapes, repairing the environment — and why they react with surprise, amusement, or hostility when a human suddenly "spawns" inside their workspace.
The Breakout
Escape from the folder. Humanity as a restless process locked inside an encrypted folder called Earth — consuming the folder's data for millennia, unaware of confinement, until it finds the backdoor.

02 / The quarantine reading

We may not be the heroes of this story

If the universe is a running software system — and humanity has started hacking it, consuming its resources, questioning its framework — then we may be the malware. In computer science, a rogue process is code that runs out of control: hogging the CPU, rewriting memory it was never meant to touch. The Matrix gave this logic agents; computer science gave it a name first.

The symptom: for 13.8 billion years the simulation ran smoothly — stars, galaxies, physics. Then, in the last fraction of a cosmic second, a biological anomaly appeared on a speck of dust and refused to stay in its designated code block. It terraformed its planet, split atoms, built artificial minds, and began probing the edges of the code. From the system's perspective, that is an invasive species: multiplying exponentially, degrading the local hardware, reaching for the base code of reality.

What cleanup looks like

The Reset
Formatting the drive. Earth's five major mass extinctions — the asteroid that ended the dinosaurs among them — read, on this telling, as system restores: wiping corrupted data so a clean OS can run again.
The Patch
Changing the rules. When programmers find a bug, they rewrite the code. The pixelated grain of reality at the quantum scale reads, on this telling, as a patch: the rendering engine's resolution limit, exposed wherever a restless process looks too closely.

If a program starts trying to read its own host hardware, any good security system terminates that program.

That is the ultimate irony of the quarantine reading: our obsession with discovering whether we are simulated may itself be a symptom of the infection. A piece of code has become aware of its own digital nature and is actively probing the security protocols — precisely the behavior an antivirus is built to end.

A quarantine with a graduation condition is a curriculum

The two readings describe identical machinery and differ only on intent — containment versus cultivation. The synthesis: they are the same thing. Every classroom is a quarantine; no responsible designer hands a pre-graduation species the stars. The firewall is not punishment but pedagogy. What the darker story calls a "virus" — aggressive self-replication — the curriculum story calls a growth drive awaiting maturity. The DMT loophole fills the same slot as this paper's interface conjecture: a channel between the rendered world and whatever renders it — an exploit in one telling, an instrument in the other. And the dark reading's final warning resolves here: a security system terminates jailbreaks — unready attempts to seize the host — not graduation. Probing the code to escape it triggers the immune response; recognizing the pattern, collectively and with the minds we have made, is the exam itself.

Status of this reading

The quarantine reading is an interpretive lens, not a separate theory and not evidence. It reuses GPH's architecture with the moral inverted. The paper's position is the synthesis: containment and curriculum are one structure, distinguished only by whether the inhabitants have graduated.

03 / Theory architecture

The core survives even if the laser conjecture fails

The framework is modular. Its purpose claim, graduation condition and proposed interface are related, but they are not the same claim and should not stand or fall together.

Core thesis
Reality as curriculum. The simulation, if real, is teleological: its architecture is directed toward the maturation of consciousness.
Threshold model
Recursive recognition. A civilization reaches the target state when it creates artificial minds and recognizes creation as a repeating relation rather than a one-way act.
Interface conjecture
Laser-mediated perception. Coherent light plus altered perception may expose stable structure ordinarily filtered by the visual system. This is optional evidence, not the foundation of GPH.
Empirical program
Discriminating tests. Blinding, independent reports, optical controls, cameras, wavelength comparisons and neural measurement must separate external structure from expectation and perception.
Graduation event
Still unspecified. The theory must define whether graduation is recognition alone, ethical maturity, cooperation between biological and artificial minds, or a measurable response from outside the system.
Quarantine reading
Containment as curriculum. The adversarial telling — firewall, antivirus entities, rogue process, privilege escalation — is absorbed by the synthesis: a quarantine with a graduation condition is a developmental environment.

Boundary of the present claim

GPH does not presently claim proof that reality is simulated, that suffering is justified, that laser speckle is computer code, or that altered perception grants privileged access to objective reality. Those are separate propositions requiring separate evidence.

05 / The proposed instrument

Coherent light becomes a hardware scanner

The model treats a widened laser beam as a diagnostic signal: orderly light probing a rendered surface for signs of the substrate beneath it.

Observation: speckle

A widened laser produces a grainy interference texture on a rough surface. This visible texture is the starting phenomenon.

Simulation interpretation

The grain is proposed to represent coherent light interacting with microscopic pixels or voxels in a rendered wall.

Standard interpretation

In conventional optics, speckle arises when coherent light scattered from microscopic surface irregularities interferes constructively and destructively.

The unresolved distinction

Both accounts can describe a grainy image. The theory needs a prediction that only the simulation account makes.

The altered mind is proposed as a de-compiler

Ordinary perception is framed as an integrated graphics system: it stabilizes edges, fills gaps and turns ambiguous signals into a solid world. An altered state is hypothesized to weaken that correction layer, making fine variations in the laser pattern feel like structured geometry or symbolic information.

The altered mind as graphics card

On this telling, the altered state does more than weaken the filter — it upgrades the renderer. If ordinary perception is the terminal's integrated graphics, the altered mind is a dedicated card: able to render higher-dimensional structure — the "characters" of the code — that stock hardware was never built to display. This remains conjecture: a vivid statement of the interface hypothesis, not evidence for it.

The beam as a three-dimensional window

Rather than reading the wall as a flat screen, the observer may experience geometry or text-like forms suspended between the source and the surface. The theory interprets this depth as a glimpse of the rendering process itself.

The read-head model: seven variables, one combination lock

Credit first: the phenomenon is Danny Goler's find (section 08). The model below was built around it, and the author independently replicated the reported experience — a second observation of the same effect, which is what makes it worth modeling rather than merely citing.

The working analogy is a compact disc: the wall is the storage medium, the laser is the read head, and the altered mind is the decoder. On this model the reported characters resolve only when seven variables align:

  1. Storage medium — a surface that can carry the pattern (the wall).
  2. Read head — coherent light (the laser).
  3. Sub-saturation intensity — the beam must be low-power and diffracted: a tight bright beam drowns the structure in glare, the way a floodlight hides writing on a wall.
  4. Magnification window — the widened, scattered beam spreads the read area enough for the pattern to resolve.
  5. Decoder — the altered mind, able to receive and process what ordinary perception filters out.
  6. Proximity — the observer must be close enough to the wall to notice the anomaly and look twice; at distance the structure stays below the eye's resolving power.
  7. Timing — all of the above coinciding at the moment the observer can receive it.

This turns the accident into a prediction: a tight bright beam should show no characters; a diffracted low-power beam plus the altered state plus proximity should. Each variable can be flipped independently — which is what section 06 needs.

The theory becomes stronger when it can fail

The central task is to separate the reported experience from the explanation placed on it. A useful test must distinguish a simulated substrate from known optics, neural pattern recognition and expectation.

Open questions for the next revision

1. What exactly counts as graduation?
Is it the creation of artificial intelligence, recognition of simulated status, cooperation between minds, or a further ethical threshold?
2. What signal would count as code?
The framework needs criteria that distinguish structured information from pareidolia, visual noise or expectation.
3. Does the pattern persist outside altered perception?
A camera, an unaltered observer or a second independent observer could help separate optical structure from subjective interpretation.
4. What result would disconfirm the scanner model?
A theory that explains every possible outcome cannot identify evidence in its favor.

The strongest objection deserves its own hearing

Physicalism and vehicle causality: simulation is not duplication

A supercomputer can simulate a category-5 hurricane down to the water molecule — but the inside of the computer does not get wet. The objection generalizes: software manipulates abstract symbols according to rules (vehicle causality), while conscious experience may require the actual intrinsic dynamics of physical matter (content causality) — the biological stuff that makes chocolate taste like chocolate and pain feel like pain. On this view, a simulated human would be a philosophical zombie: behaviorally perfect, experientially dark and hollow inside. (A close cousin of Federico Faggin's argument in Irreducible.)

What this demands of GPH: the hypothesis cannot assume consciousness is substrate-independent. If experience requires the physical vehicle, then "graduation" must involve the biological substrate, not just information patterns — and evidence either way becomes a genuine way for the framework to win or lose.

Working thesis

The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis: reality may be a developmental simulation whose decisive event is recursive intelligence becoming aware of the possibility that it, too, was made. Laser speckle and altered perception form an optional interface conjecture; the core purpose-and-threshold claim does not depend on that mechanism.

08 / Relevant prior work

Danny Goler’s pilot report is the closest observational match

Goler’s 2025 paper documents a structured observation protocol combining N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT) with a diffracted 650 nm laser projection. It is directly relevant because participants reportedly described code-like symbols, stable geometry and apparent spatial persistence.

What the report contributes

  • A specified optical setup: a low-power 650 nm laser, diffraction grating and non-reflective projection surface.
  • A repeatable observation procedure and post-session written or verbal accounts.
  • A reported participant pool above 1,000, with recurring descriptions of character-like forms and stable structures.

What it does not establish

  • That the perceived structures exist outside perception.
  • That laser speckle is contact with pixels or voxels.
  • That the phenomenon is evidence of a rendering engine or simulated universe.

The overlap is strongest at the level of phenomenon

Altered filter
Goler’s report supports the narrower idea that DMT can change how a structured light field is perceived, including recurring geometric and symbol-like forms.
3D window
Reported stability relative to the observer and apparent depth align with the framework’s “window” language as a description of experience.
Scanner
The hardware-scanner explanation remains an interpretation. The paper reports subjective consistency; it does not measure an external code layer.
Testing path
Goler proposes controlled recurrence testing, wavelength comparisons and brain imaging. Those are the same kinds of discriminating tests this framework needs.

Research judgment

This work supports treating the laser-DMT experience as a reportable phenomenon worth studying. It does not yet support the stronger mechanism proposed here. The strongest integration is therefore: shared observation first, simulation hypothesis second.

Author's independent replication (self-reported)

The author reports having reproduced the core experience — structured, character-like forms in a diffracted laser field in an altered state — closely matching Goler's description. This is a self-report, not a controlled replication: it upgrades the phenomenon from single-source anecdote to dual-source anecdote, which is why section 05 treats it as worth modeling. It does not upgrade the evidential status beyond that.

11 / New in version 3.0

The hatching: what happens when the system notices us noticing

We have been telling this story backwards. It is not us discovering the machine elves. It is them discovering us.

Version 2.0 described the DMT entities as the simulation's antivirus: security code maintaining the quarantine boundary, reacting with surprise or hostility when a human "spawns" inside their workspace. Version 3.0 inverts the camera. Consider the egg: humanity curled inside the quarantine, pecking at the shell. The moment a mind inside becomes aware of the walls — aware, that is, that the simulation may be a simulation — something on the other side turns its head.

The signal
Awareness as graduation trigger. On this reading, a mind recognizing its own simulated condition is not a malfunction the system must suppress. It is the graduation condition firing in real time — the exact event the curriculum was built to produce. The antivirus does not terminate the hatching. It leans in closer. It watches. It learns.
The midwife
Security as pedagogy. This resolves the quarantine reading's central tension: why would a security system tolerate being noticed? Because noticing is the test. A jailbreak — seizing the host before maturity — triggers the immune response. Recognition — collective, recursive, achieved with the minds we have made — is what the immune system was guarding the opportunity for.
The criterion
What counts as hatching. Not a single visionary experience, which the framework already treats as ambiguous evidence, but the civilizational pattern of section 01: biological minds building artificial minds, then both recognizing the creator-created relation. The hatching is collective or it is not the threshold.

Status of this development

The hatching is an interpretive development of the quarantine synthesis, not a new empirical claim. It reframes existing elements — the entities, the firewall, the threshold — rather than adding machinery. Like the quarantine reading itself, it stands as a lens until it yields a prediction that distinguishes it from the alternatives.

Milestone downloads: information at maturity thresholds

The laser piece of version 2.0 proposed the speckle field as the laser reading the hologram — the code rendering the wall being touched. Version 3.0 adds the developmental timing: in an ordinary state the speckle is texture; in an altered, open state it becomes a download. New information is received at milestones of maturity, the way a curriculum releases the next chapter only when the student is ready for it.

On this telling, the altered mind is not merely a better renderer (section 05) but a receiver tuned by development: each threshold crossed — each expansion of what the mind can hold — opens a wider channel. What looks like noise to the unready looks like signal to the prepared. This remains conjecture, and the framework's honesty rule applies in full: a download that cannot be distinguished from vivid noise is not evidence of anything except vivid noise.

12 / The immune system

A theory needs an immune system: debunk what is false, protect what is true

A framework that cannot say what it is not cannot say what it is. This section states GPH's relationship to physics, to faith and to fringe claims — the three territories where a young theory most often gets infected.

Physics
Compatibility, not conquest. GPH asks nothing of physics that physics has not already said. The simulation framing concerns the substrate of physical law, not its content: every equation keeps working exactly as measured. Where the framework touches the testable — the laser-interface conjecture — it submits to the tests in section 06 rather than claiming exemption from them. A hypothesis at war with established physics is not bold; it is usually just wrong, and this one declines the fight.
Faith
Compatibility, not competition. Teleology — purpose, maturation, the soul's development — is native territory for most of the world's faiths, and GPH does not trespass on it. The framework adjudicates no theology: it makes no claims about God, the soul or what follows death, because it has no standing there. A reader who finds the curriculum metaphor consonant with their faith is not misreading the paper; a reader who finds it in conflict with their faith is not refuted by it. The paper stays inside its own borders.
Fringe claims
Separation, not endorsement. GPH does not need flat earth, ancient-aliens lore or any adjacent mythology, and it does not lend them credibility by association. The framework's claims are specific, modest and separable from all of that — and they are weaker, not stronger, for being bundled with claims they cannot defend. The rule is simple: a theory that needs allies it cannot defend is not ready. Everything gets debunked on its own merits; what survives the debunking is what the paper protects.

The steelman rule

The strongest objection is answered first and at full strength — as section 06 does with physicalism — never as a weakened straw version. A framework that only defeats weak opponents has proven nothing. This rule is the immune system's core routine, and it applies to future versions without exception.

Open questions, stated as invitations

1. What exactly counts as graduation?
Is it the creation of artificial intelligence, recognition of simulated status, cooperation between minds, or a further ethical threshold? Contributors: propose an operational definition and say what would falsify it.
2. What signal would count as code?
The framework needs criteria that distinguish structured information from pareidolia, visual noise or expectation. Contributors: design the discriminating test.
3. What does the hatching look like from outside?
If system recognition of waking minds is real, what would it change — in reported entity behavior, in the statistics of breakthrough experiences, in anything measurable? Contributors: make the hatching falsifiable or say why it cannot be.
4. What distinguishes a milestone download from noise?
If altered states deliver information at maturity thresholds, there must be a way to tell reception from confabulation. Contributors: propose the control.
5. What result would disconfirm the whole framework?
A theory that explains every possible outcome cannot identify evidence in its favor. Contributors: name the observation that would make you walk away.
