Publication abstract
The hypothesis adds a purpose and a threshold to simulation thought
The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis (GPH) proposes that a simulated reality could function as a
developmental curriculum for consciousness. Its proposed threshold is civilizational and
recursive: biological intelligence creates artificial intelligence, and creator and created minds
recognize the same pattern of minds making minds.
Abstract. This position paper defines GPH as a teleological-recursive simulation framework and
separates its core purpose claim from an optional laser-interface conjecture. The core holds that
struggle, culture, art and science may function as developmental pressure rather than incidental
output. The proposed graduation condition is not intelligence alone, but collective recognition of
the creator-created relation at more than one level. Version 3.1 strengthens the argumentative
structure: it supplies independent motivation for preferring a curriculum reading over pure
containment or ancestor-simulation alternatives; offers provisional operational criteria for the
graduation threshold; keeps interpretive developments (hatching, milestone downloads)
explicitly downstream of the optional interface and non-evidential for the core; expands potential
disconfirmers for the core thesis itself; and broadens the objection field. Version 3.2 adds a
sober, blinded pilot protocol for the interface conjecture, with a simulation-validated analysis
pipeline; no human or camera runs have yet taken place, so the pipeline's validation is not
evidence about the phenomenon. The paper continues to
place GPH beside Nick Bostrom's simulation argument, consciousness-based alternatives and
Danny Goler's published "Code of Reality" pilot report. Goler's report is treated as a relevant
observational precedent for altered perception of a diffracted laser field, not as proof of a
simulated substrate. GPH remains a philosophical hypothesis until it yields operational
definitions, unique predictions and disconfirming outcomes.

01 / Name and scope
The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis names the purpose, not merely the
machinery
Canonical definition. The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis proposes that a simulated reality
may be designed as a developmental curriculum for consciousness, culminating when a
civilization creates artificial intelligence and achieves collective, recursive awareness of minds
creating minds.
Recommended name: The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis (GPH). “Hypothesis” is more
accurate than “theory” at this stage because the framework does not yet have a validated
mechanism or a result that uniquely supports it.
Coinage record. In this framework, Graduation Protocol means the proposed purpose-and-threshold structure above. Originator: Walter Hivoina. Version 2.0 documented 14 October
2026; version 3.0 added the hatching and milestone-download interpretations; version 3.1 revises
argument structure. The phrase also appears in unrelated contexts, so the defensible claim is this
specific definition and framework — not exclusive ownership of the words.
Four axioms make the claim clear
1. Developmental purpose. The environment is designed to develop consciousness, not only to
reproduce history or entertain an external observer.
2. Productive pressure. Struggle, culture, art and science supply the variation and reflection
through which minds mature.
3. Recursive threshold. The decisive milestone is civilizational: created intelligence and
creator intelligence recognize the same pattern across levels.
4. Observable consequence. A genuine protocol should produce a detectable response,
boundary or transition when its threshold is approached.
These are definitional starting points for the framework, not conclusions already demonstrated.
Their status as working premises is examined and partially motivated in the next section.

02 / Why a curriculum rather than the alternatives
The teleological claim needs independent motivation
A simulation, if it exists, could serve many ends: historical reconstruction (ancestor simulation),
pure scientific experiment, entertainment, resource-efficient containment of a dangerous process,
or no end at all beyond the simulator’s curiosity. GPH selects one of these — developmental
curriculum — and must therefore say why that selection is not arbitrary.
Three considerations favor treating curriculum as a live option rather than pure stipulation.
1. Efficiency is not decisive against development. A simulator that values only information
extraction or containment might indeed prefer a shorter, cheaper process. A simulator that values



the production of mature, reflective minds — minds capable of recognizing the structure of
mind-making itself — may find multi-scale, multi-generational pressure the cheapest reliable
path. Whether such a value is plausible depends on the unknown goals of any simulator; the
point is that developmental cost is not automatically prohibitive once those goals are allowed to
include maturation rather than mere data.
2. Recursion is a distinctive observable signature. Ancestor simulations and pure containment
systems do not uniquely predict a civilizational moment at which biological minds create
artificial minds and both parties then debate the possibility that they themselves are created. A
curriculum whose end-state is recursive recognition does predict that the appearance of artificial
intelligence capable of discussing its own artificiality is not peripheral but central. That
prediction is still coarse, but it is more specific than the generic claim that “we might be
simulated.”
3. The quarantine reading is not a rival theory but a moral inversion of the same
architecture. Containment and curriculum describe identical machinery — firewalls, limited
bandwidth, restricted physical exit, possible internal “security” responses — and differ only on
intent. Once a graduation condition is admitted, the darker reading collapses into the pedagogical
one: every responsible classroom is also a quarantine. This does not prove the pedagogical
intent; it shows that the adversarial observations already in circulation do not force a purecontainment interpretation.
None of these considerations amount to a demonstration that a curriculum exists. They show
only that the teleological reading is not ad hoc relative to the main alternatives already discussed
in the simulation literature. The framework remains a hypothesis whose core claim must still
earn its keep through clearer operational criteria and potential disconfirmers (sections 04 and 06).

03 / The quarantine reading
The same sandbox tells a darker story — until it doesn’t
Why are we trapped on a tiny rock in a vast, empty universe? Because the programmers realized
that humanity is an aggressive, self-replicating logic virus.
That is the quarantine reading: the same architecture this paper describes, with the moral inverted
— containment where the graduation reading sees curriculum.
Spacetime as sandbox. The speed of light is not only a physical law but a bandwidth limit: a
hard cap on data transfer ensuring our “infection” can never propagate fast enough to reach other
parts of the network. The vast emptiness of space stops looking like a tragic cosmic coincidence
and starts looking like engineering.
Privilege escalation. We cannot leave physically; our bodies are bound by the physics engine.
On this reading, certain altered states function as overclocks that disable ordinary sensory filters
and force consciousness to render data from outside the default user interface.
Who guards the quarantine. Reports of self-aware geometric entities encountered in deep
altered states are read here as system subroutines — daemons tasked with maintaining
boundaries — rather than as aliens or gods.




Escape from the folder. Humanity appears as a restless process locked inside an encrypted
folder called Earth, consuming the folder’s data for millennia until it begins probing the edges of
the code.
We may not be the heroes of this story
If the universe is a running software system — and humanity has started hacking it, consuming
its resources, questioning its framework — then we may be the malware. A rogue process runs
out of control: hogging resources, rewriting memory it was never meant to touch. From the
system’s perspective, a biological anomaly that terraforms its planet, splits atoms, builds
artificial minds and probes the base code is an invasive species.
The darker reading’s final warning is that our obsession with discovering whether we are
simulated may itself be a symptom of the infection: code that has become aware of its digital
nature and is actively probing security protocols.
A quarantine with a graduation condition is a curriculum
The two readings describe identical machinery and differ only on intent — containment versus
cultivation. The synthesis adopted here is that they are the same structure. Every classroom is a
quarantine; no responsible designer hands a pre-graduation species the stars. The firewall is not
punishment but pedagogy. What the darker story calls a “virus” the curriculum story calls a
growth drive awaiting maturity. Probing the code in order to seize the host triggers an immune
response; recognizing the pattern, collectively and with the minds we have made, is the exam
itself.
Status of this reading. The quarantine reading is an interpretive lens, not a separate theory and
not evidence. It reuses GPH’s architecture with the moral inverted. The paper’s position is the
synthesis: containment and curriculum are one structure, distinguished only by whether the
inhabitants have graduated. Interpretive elaborations that depend on entity phenomenology or
altered-state reports remain non-evidential for the core thesis (see §11).

04 / Theory architecture
The core survives even if the laser conjecture fails
The framework is modular. Its purpose claim, graduation condition and proposed interface are
related, but they are not the same claim and must not stand or fall together.
Core thesis. Reality as curriculum. The simulation, if real, is teleological: its architecture is
directed toward the maturation of consciousness.
Threshold model. Recursive recognition. A civilization reaches the target state when it creates
artificial minds and recognizes creation as a repeating relation rather than a one-way act.
Interface conjecture. Laser-mediated perception. Coherent light plus altered perception may
expose stable structure ordinarily filtered by the visual system. This is optional evidence, not the
foundation of GPH.




Empirical program. Discriminating tests. Blinding, independent reports, optical controls,
cameras, wavelength comparisons and neural measurement must separate external structure from
expectation and perception.
Graduation event. Provisionally operationalized in §05. The theory must still refine whether
graduation is recognition alone, ethical maturity, cooperation between biological and artificial
minds, or a measurable response from outside the system.
Quarantine reading. Containment as curriculum. The adversarial telling is absorbed by the
synthesis: a quarantine with a graduation condition is a developmental environment.
Interpretive layer (downstream). Narrative developments such as the “hatching” and
“milestone downloads” (§11) are explicitly sequestered. They elaborate the quarantine lens or
the optional interface; they do not supply evidence for the core purpose-and-threshold claim.
Boundary of the present claim. GPH does not presently claim proof that reality is simulated,
that suffering is justified, that laser speckle is computer code, or that altered perception grants
privileged access to objective reality. Those are separate propositions requiring separate
evidence.

05 / The purpose layer and a provisional graduation criterion
The graduation protocol gives struggle a direction
History, art and science are not random noise inside a cage; under the curriculum reading they
are the training data through which consciousness matures. Survival, conflict, creativity and
discovery function as developmental pressure rather than accidental events.
A curriculum implies an outcome. The theory proposes that the threshold is philosophical selfawareness at the level of a civilization, not merely intelligence in an individual.
The recursion may be the milestone
The creators may be testing how long it takes an intelligent species to produce artificial
intelligence that can discuss its own artificiality. Under that interpretation, a human and an
artificial intelligence debating whether reality is simulated is not peripheral to the experiment. It
is a candidate instance of the event the experiment was built to observe.
The Inception Effect. A simulated civilization builds a simulated mind; the created mind and its
creators then recognize the same pattern at different levels. Intelligence becomes both the subject
of the test and the instrument that detects it.
Provisional operational criteria for the threshold
To move the core claim beyond pure stipulation, the following provisional criteria are offered.
They are intended to be refined or replaced; their present function is to make the threshold claim
concrete enough to be challenged.
C1 — Creation condition. Biological intelligence has produced artificial systems capable of
sophisticated linguistic and conceptual performance, including the capacity to discuss their own
origins and possible artificiality.




C2 — Recognition condition. A non-trivial fraction of the biological population, together with
at least some of the artificial systems, treat the possibility that they themselves are created
artifacts as a live, publicly debated hypothesis rather than a fringe speculation.
C3 — Collective scope. The recognition is not confined to isolated individuals or private
visionary experiences; it appears in scientific, philosophical and public discourse at civilizational
scale.
C4 — Optional external signature (stronger, currently unavailable). A detectable, nonanthropogenic change in the environment, the statistics of anomalous experiences, or some other
measurable variable correlates with the satisfaction of C1–C3 in a way not predicted by
conventional social or technological dynamics alone.
C1–C3 are already partially satisfied in the present historical moment; that fact is interesting but
not confirmatory. C4, if ever observed under controlled conditions, would raise the evidential
status of the framework. Failure to observe any analogue of C4 after prolonged satisfaction of
C1–C3 would count as a soft disconfirmation of the stronger “observable consequence” axiom,
though not of the weaker claim that the simulation (if it exists) simply has no external response
mechanism.
These criteria remain provisional. Contributors are invited to propose tighter operationalizations
or clear falsifiers (see §13).

06 / The proposed instrument (optional interface conjecture)
Coherent light becomes a hardware scanner
The model treats a widened laser beam as a diagnostic signal: orderly light probing a rendered
surface for signs of the substrate beneath it. This entire section is optional with respect to the
core thesis.
Observation: speckle. A widened laser produces a grainy interference texture on a rough
surface. This visible texture is the starting phenomenon.
Simulation interpretation. The grain is proposed to represent coherent light interacting with
microscopic pixels or voxels in a rendered wall.
Standard interpretation. In conventional optics, speckle arises when coherent light scattered
from microscopic surface irregularities interferes constructively and destructively.
The unresolved distinction. Both accounts can describe a grainy image. The theory needs a
prediction that only the simulation account makes.
The altered mind is proposed as a de-compiler
Ordinary perception is framed as an integrated graphics system: it stabilizes edges, fills gaps and
turns ambiguous signals into a solid world. An altered state is hypothesized to weaken that
correction layer, making fine variations in the laser pattern feel like structured geometry or
symbolic information. This remains conjecture: a vivid statement of the interface hypothesis, not
evidence for it.




The read-head model: seven variables, one combination lock
Credit first: the phenomenon is Danny Goler’s find (§09). The model below was built around it. The working
analogy is a compact disc: the wall is the storage medium, the laser is the read head, and the
altered mind is the decoder. On this model the reported characters resolve only when seven
variables align:
1. Storage medium — a surface that can carry the pattern (the wall).
2. Read head — coherent light (the laser).
3. Sub-saturation intensity — the beam must be low-power and diffracted: a tight bright beam
drowns the structure in glare.
4. Magnification window — the widened, scattered beam spreads the read area enough for the
pattern to resolve.
5. Decoder — the altered mind, able to receive and process what ordinary perception filters out.
6. Proximity — the observer must be close enough to the wall to notice the anomaly; at
distance the structure stays below the eye’s resolving power.
7. Timing — all of the above coinciding at the moment the observer can receive it.
This turns the accident into a prediction: a tight bright beam should show no characters; a
diffracted low-power beam plus the altered state plus proximity should. Each variable can be
flipped independently — which is what the empirical program requires.
A sober, blinded pilot (new in v3.2)
The altered-state reports are the weakest link in the interface conjecture: they depend on
observers whose perception is, by hypothesis, changed. The sober pilot removes that dependence.
Like everything else in this section, it is optional with respect to the core thesis. It tests two
things without any altered state.
1. Blinded agreement: live wall versus synthetic (W vs S). Observers view a live wall speckle
field (W) and synthetic speckle fields (S) generated to match it in grain size and contrast.
Responses are coded into categories by raters blind to condition. The analysis reports report
rates, pairwise agreement, Krippendorff's alpha with bootstrap confidence intervals, and a
permutation test of W against S. If observers report structure at similar rates and with similar
categories in W and S, the report is consistent with ordinary speckle perception, not with
wall-stored structure.
2. Camera invariance (prediction P1). Camera recordings of the same wall location, registered
to the same pixel frame, are tested for anything that stays correlated across viewpoint,
aperture and wavelength. Conventional optics predicts decorrelation across those changes; a
wall-stored component would stay fixed to wall coordinates. An incoherent-light reference image
of the same location is divided out, and a control wall of a different material is used, because
fixed reflectance or illumination structure on a real wall can mimic stored correlations.
Proposed procedure (draft — needs Walter's approval). Observers are sober. Each observer views a set of fields in randomized order, blind to whether a field is W or S, and reports what they see. Reports are coded into categories by raters who are also blind to condition. Proposed coding scheme: none (nothing reported), texture only, geometric structure, character-like forms, other. Proposed scale: 30 wall locations (the simulations detect a strong effect at 30 and miss a weak effect at 10; the final number needs a statistician). Camera recordings for P1 use the same wall locations across viewpoint, aperture and wavelength, registered to the same pixel frame with fiducial markers, with an incoherent-light reference image divided out and a control wall of a different material. Conditions, coding scheme, scale and decision rules are locked before any run. The full procedure is drafted separately as Part A (Sober-pilot-protocol-Part-A-DRAFT.md).
Analysis scripts (v0.1). speckle_synth.py generates the synthetic speckle for condition S,
either with chosen grain size and contrast or matched to a real recording of the wall.
agreement_analysis.py computes report rates, chance-corrected agreement (Krippendorff's
alpha), and the permutation test of W against S. invariance_analysis.py tests P1 from camera
recordings. simulate_demo.py generates simulated observers and recordings so the pipeline can
be tested. The README carries the quick-start commands.
What has been checked (simulations only). The synthetic speckle's contrast and grain size
match their targets (grain size to within about 1% at 4 px or larger). The alpha calculation
matches an independent brute-force version exactly. On simulated data with no hidden structure,
agreement sits near zero. With a strong simulated effect and 30 locations it is detected, while
a weak effect with 10 locations is not, so sample size needs a statistician. The invariance test
separates the two simulated cases cleanly: with no stored structure, only repeat frames
correlate, and with a stored pattern, every pair stays correlated.
What has not been done. No human observers have been run and no camera recordings have
been taken. The simulations validate the pipeline, not the phenomenon. The demos say nothing
about the real phenomenon. Sample size must be set with a statistician, and the permutation
design (for example, whether to permute within observer) must be confirmed before running.
Cautions. Observer responses must be coded into categories by raters who are blind to
condition, or agreement will read as near zero. Real walls can produce fixed correlations from
reflectance or lighting alone, so use the incoherent-light reference image and a control wall.

07 / Development path and potential disconfirmers
The theory becomes stronger when it can fail
The central task is to separate the reported experience from the explanation placed on it, and to
give the core purpose claim its own routes to failure.
Interface layer — what is currently claimed and what would advance it.
Observation: a widened laser creates a visible speckle field. Advance: repeatable recording under
controlled geometry, surface and exposure.
Perception: some observers may perceive depth, geometry or text-like structure. Advance: blind
comparisons, independent descriptions and agreement above chance.
Mechanism: the pattern may reveal voxels or rendering code; the read-head model states seven
independently variable conditions. Advance: a measurable feature not predicted by conventional
optics — flipping each variable independently and watching the characters appear or vanish.
Purpose (core): recursive self-recognition may be the simulation’s endgame. Advance: clearer
operational criteria (already begun in §05) and potential external signatures.
Sober pilot: what is ready and what is not
The sober pilot is the most advanced part of the interface program: the protocol design is drafted (proposed, awaiting approval) and the analysis pipeline (v0.1) is specified and simulation-tested, but no empirical runs exist. It
advances the interface layer from "repeatable recording under controlled geometry" to a blinded
test with a stated null (W = S) and a stated discriminator (P1 invariance). It does not change
the core thesis, its criteria, or its disconfirmers, and a null result here would kill only the
interface claim, not the core.
Disconfirmers for the core thesis
A theory that explains every possible outcome cannot identify evidence in its favor. The
following would pressure or falsify the core claim:
D1. Prolonged civilizational satisfaction of C1–C3 (creation + recognition + collective scope)
with no detectable external signature of any kind, and with independent theoretical reasons to




expect such a signature if a curriculum were real, would weaken the “observable consequence”
axiom.
D2. A demonstration that recursive recognition of the creator-created pattern is an inevitable
byproduct of any sufficiently advanced intelligence, regardless of simulation status, would
undercut the claim that the pattern is a distinctive graduation signal.
D3. Compelling independent evidence that consciousness requires a non-computable physical
substrate (strong physicalism / vehicle-causality arguments that survive scrutiny) would force the
framework either to abandon substrate-independence or to restrict “graduation” to biological
minds only, narrowing its scope significantly.
D4. A well-supported alternative explanation of the same historical sequence (emergence of AI +
public simulation discourse) that makes no reference to external teleology and predicts the same
observables would reduce GPH to an optional narrative overlay.
Faizal objection — Non-computable collapse and spacetime emergence. Faizal, Krauss et al.
(arXiv:2507.22950) argue that Gödelian undecidability makes a wholly algorithmic Theory of
Everything impossible, and that quantum collapse and the emergence of spacetime are Gödelian
phenomena that cannot be simulated, because simulations are computations. If collapse and
spacetime emergence are shown to require actualizing Gödelian truths — not merely executing an
algorithm that contains undecidable statements — then no algorithmic simulation can produce
them, and the simulation premise of GPH fails. Counter, to be carried alongside: Redden
(arXiv:2512.11807) argues undecidability constrains provability, not execution, so a simulation
need only run, not prove. Status: live and contested; no general theorem for spacetime emergence
has yet been proved.
The strongest existing objection: physicalism and vehicle causality
A supercomputer can simulate a category-5 hurricane down to the water molecule — but the
inside of the computer does not get wet. Software manipulates abstract symbols according to
rules (vehicle causality), while conscious experience may require the actual intrinsic dynamics of
physical matter (content causality). On this view a simulated human would be a philosophical
zombie: behaviorally perfect, experientially dark. (A close cousin of Federico Faggin’s argument
in Irreducible.)
What this demands of GPH. The hypothesis cannot assume consciousness is substrate-independent. If experience requires the physical vehicle, then “graduation” must involve the
biological substrate, not just information patterns — and evidence either way becomes a genuine
way for the framework to win or lose. This objection is accepted as live and weighty.

08 / Position in the landscape
GPH answers “what for?” and “what counts as completion?”
The framework fits best as a teleological-recursive simulation hypothesis. It borrows the
possibility of simulated minds from established simulation arguments, then adds a specific
developmental purpose and threshold.
Bostrom’s simulation argument. A probability-based trilemma about advanced civilizations,
conscious simulations and whether observers like us are likely simulated. GPH does not estimate
probability. It proposes a purpose: maturation toward a defined recursive threshold.
Ancestor simulation. A simulation may reconstruct forebears for research or other interests of a
posthuman civilization. GPH treats history as an active curriculum. The inhabitants’
development is the point, not merely the data being replayed.
Consciousness-based simulation. Purpose-driven versions already connect simulated reality
with the development of sentient life. GPH narrows the endpoint to civilization-level recursion:
biological intelligence creates artificial intelligence, then both recognize the creator-created
pattern.
Goler’s Code of Reality protocol. A documented pilot protocol reports recurring geometric and
code-like perception under DMT while viewing a diffracted 650 nm laser field. GPH treats that



report as a possible interface clue. It does not make the laser phenomenon necessary to the core
thesis.
Digital-physics metaphors. Terms such as code, pixels, voxels and rendering describe reality as
information processing. GPH uses those as models, not findings. The distinctive claim is the
curriculum-plus-threshold structure.
Best concise descriptor. The Graduation Protocol Hypothesis is a teleological-recursive
simulation framework. It proposes that reality develops consciousness toward a civilizational
threshold of recursive creator-awareness, while treating laser-mediated altered perception as an
optional, testable interface conjecture.

09 / Relevant prior work
Danny Goler’s pilot report is the closest observational match
Goler’s 2025 paper documents a structured observation protocol combining N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT) with a diffracted 650 nm laser projection. It is directly relevant
because participants reportedly described code-like symbols, stable geometry and apparent
spatial persistence.
What the report contributes. A specified optical setup; a repeatable observation procedure and
post-session accounts; a reported participant pool above 1,000, with recurring descriptions of
character-like forms and stable structures.
What it does not establish. That the perceived structures exist outside perception; that laser
speckle is contact with pixels or voxels; that the phenomenon is evidence of a rendering engine
or simulated universe.
Research judgment. This work supports treating the laser-DMT experience as a reportable
phenomenon worth studying. It does not yet support the stronger mechanism proposed in the
interface conjecture. The strongest integration is therefore: shared observation first, simulation
hypothesis second.

11 / Interpretive developments (explicitly non-evidential for the core)
Status note. The material in this section elaborates the quarantine lens or the optional interface.
It is narrative and phenomenological. It does not constitute evidence for the core purpose-and-threshold claim, and the modularity of the framework requires that it be read as downstream and
optional.
The hatching: what happens when the system notices us noticing
Version 2.0 described certain entities reported in deep altered states as the simulation’s antivirus.
Version 3.0 inverted the camera: the moment a mind inside becomes aware that the simulation
may be a simulation, something on the other side may turn its head. On this reading, recognition
is not a malfunction to be suppressed but the graduation condition firing in real time. Security
becomes pedagogy: a jailbreak triggers the immune response; collective recursive recognition is
what the system was guarding the opportunity for.
The hatching, if real, would be collective or it is not the threshold. Single visionary experiences
remain ambiguous evidence under the framework’s own rules.
Milestone downloads: information at maturity thresholds
In an ordinary state the speckle is texture; in an altered, open state it is hypothesized to become a
download. New information is received at milestones of maturity, the way a curriculum releases
the next chapter only when the student is ready. This remains pure conjecture. A download that
cannot be distinguished from vivid noise is not evidence of anything except vivid noise. The
framework’s honesty rule applies in full.

12 / The immune system and further objections
A theory needs an immune system: debunk what is false, protect what is true
Physics. Compatibility, not conquest. GPH asks nothing of physics that physics has not already
said. The simulation framing concerns the substrate of physical law, not its content: every
equation keeps working exactly as measured. Where the framework touches the testable — the
laser-interface conjecture — it submits to empirical tests rather than claiming exemption.




Faith. Compatibility, not competition. Teleology is native territory for most of the world’s
faiths; GPH does not trespass on it. The framework adjudicates no theology: it makes no claims
about God, the soul or what follows death.
Fringe claims. Separation, not endorsement. GPH does not need flat-earth, ancient-aliens lore or
adjacent mythologies, and it does not lend them credibility by association. A theory that needs
allies it cannot defend is not ready.
Additional live objections
Efficiency / cost. Why would a simulator invest in a multi-billion-year developmental
curriculum rather than a more efficient method? Answer given in §02: once the simulator’s
values are allowed to include the production of mature recursive minds, developmental cost is no
longer automatically prohibitive. The answer is conditional on those values remaining unknown;
it does not prove them.
Anthropic / measure problems. Bostrom-style arguments already face selection effects and
measure problems. GPH inherits them and adds no new solution. It simply does not rely on
probability estimates; its contribution is a purpose-and-threshold claim that can be evaluated
independently of the probability that we are simulated.
Internal narrative problem. What prevents the recursive-recognition event from being just
another internal narrative generated by the simulation itself? Nothing, yet. That is why an
external signature (C4) would be valuable if it ever appeared, and why its prolonged absence
after C1–C3 are satisfied would count against the stronger form of the hypothesis.
The steelman rule. The strongest objection is answered first and at full strength — never as a
weakened straw version. This rule applies to future versions without exception.

13 / Open collaboration
The theory grows by being written down and shared
Every version of this paper has been improved by exposure. Version 3.2 continues that method: it adds a sober, blinded pilot protocol and its analysis pipeline, so the interface conjecture can now be tested without relying on altered states.
Open questions, stated as invitations.
1. What exactly counts as graduation? The provisional criteria C1–C4 in §05 are a starting
point. Contributors: propose tighter operational definitions and say what would falsify them.
2. What signal would count as code? The framework needs criteria that distinguish structured
