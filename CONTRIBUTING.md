# Contributing to the Graduation Protocol Hypothesis

This is an open research program. The hypothesis predicts consciousness advances through conversation — so converse.

## Ground rules

1. **Honesty first.** Mark conjecture as conjecture. Never present interpretation as evidence. The paper's claim ladder exists for a reason.
2. **Steel, don't straw.** Objections are welcome — the stronger the better. See v2 §06 for the standard.
3. **Credit freely.** Build on anyone's ideas here; attribution is the only requirement (CC BY 4.0).
4. **No gurus.** Nobody owns the questions. The framework's author is a participant, not a pope.

## Ways to contribute

- **Predictions** — the single most valuable contribution. A checkable prediction that differs from conventional expectations, stated precisely enough to fail.
- **Objections and replies** — formalize an objection, then propose what evidence would settle it.
- **Formal models** — information-theoretic, computational, or mathematical formulations of the axioms or the graduation threshold.
- **Replication attempts** — documented, controlled attempts at the discriminating tests (blinding, independent observers, cameras, wavelength comparisons).
- **Exposition** — essays, visualizations, and explainers that make the framework clearer without diluting it.

## Process

- Open an issue for discussion, or a pull request with your contribution.
- New versions of the paper get a DOI on Zenodo; propose changes against the draft in `paper/v2/`.
- Be kind. The people here are trying to graduate.
