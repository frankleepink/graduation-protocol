# Manual for Manifestation

by Walter Hivoina

1. Breathe in (2 seconds, through the nose). Picture molecules being drawn in from all life around you, in whatever environment you like. I usually hover just above the atmosphere, like the man in da Vinci's art.

2. Breathe out (3 to 4 seconds, through the mouth). Send the breath all the way down to your toes and up to your head, pulling all stress, pain, and bad energy into the exhale.

3. Repeat. Take as many breaths as needed until you feel your energy field ground or shift in any positive way.

4. Align. Visualize your chakras aligning. Feel the hairs on your body slowly rise as waves of energizing chills move all over you, and your aura begins to regenerate.

5. Shield. As your aura pulses outward, in for 2 seconds and out for 3 to 4 seconds, picture white bursts of protection shields forming around you.

6. Expand. Push your aura outward until it extends 6 to 10 feet around you. This powerful, positive frequency empowers you to control your intentions wherever you place your focus.

7. Program mode. You are now in program mode. Intend lucid dreams.

8. Set your recognition cue. In your first few lucid dreams, you may not be fully aware. Give yourself an act or phrase to recognize the dream. Ask yourself, "Am I lucid?" or "Am I dreaming?" or "Someone please pinch me!"

9. Choose your safe word or act. It can be as simple as seeing a picture by an artist you love, or a music album cover, something from your heart. Once you know it, my friend, you are there.

10. Flight. Be aware: flight can be overwhelming at first. Jumping into full flight is easy, but then come the questions: Can I land? How high can I go? How fast? Can I produce sonic booms? Can I stop?

11. Don't give in to fear. Fear may end the dream early. If you start to feel fear, turn the power down. Try leaping or bounding great distances, effortlessly, until you have the controls down for full flight.

12. Pass it on. Enjoy this manual that has been lost. Help expand it, and teach your loved ones.
